# ROBOTIC-TT-BALL-SHOOTER

**AIM:**
TO DESIGN AND ASSEMBLE A MULTIPLE DEGREE OF FREEDOM ROBOTIC TABLE TENNIS BALL SHOOTER, WITH THREE RUBBER ROLLERS (2 FOR SPEED AND 1 FOR SPIN).
INCLUDING A 60 BALL STORAGE BASKET AND A LOADING PIPE (FROM BASKET TO SHOOTER), ASSEMBLED ON A 6-WHEEL BASE FOR EASY PLACEMENT.

**DESIGN APPROACH:**
CREATING A ROBOTIC DEVICE WITH 3 LINEAR ARMS WITH ROTATION ACTUATORS AT ENDS TO ADJUST HEIGHT OF SHOOTER, 2 REVOLUTE JOINTS AT THE BASE OF ARMS AND AT THE SHOOTER TO ADJUST DIRECTION, 1 ROTATIONAL ACTUATOR AT THE END OF SHOOTER TO ADJUST SHOOTING ANGLE. APART FROM THIS, THE SHOOTER USES A 3 ROLLER SYSTEM (INSTEAD OF THE TRADITIONAL 2 ROLLER DESIGNED ONLY FOR SPPED) TO PROVIDE CONTROL OVER SPEED AND SPIN ON THE BALL, TO ACHIEVE ACCURATE PLAYER CAPABILITIES.
